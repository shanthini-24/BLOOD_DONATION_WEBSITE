Why Donate Blood?

Blood cannot be manufactured synthetically. It can only come from generous voluntary donors like you.

Saves Multiple Lives

A single pint of blood can be separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets, which can help save up to three different patients undergoing surgery, trauma recovery, or cancer care.

Health Checkup

Prior to donating, your pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels are checked for free. It gives you immediate insights into your cardiovascular health.

Stimulates Blood Cell Production

After blood donation, your body works to replenish the lost blood volume with fresh red blood cells within weeks, helping maintain vital overall organ function.

About Blood Donation

Understanding eligibility, blood group compatibility, and the vital role blood donors play in emergency healthcare.

Donor Eligibility Criteria

Age

18 to 65 years old

First-time donors must be under 60 years of age.

Weight

Minimum 45 kg (100 lbs)

Ensures safe volume removal relative to body mass.

Hemoglobin

Minimum 12.5 g/dL

Quick preliminary finger-prick test conducted onsite.

Frequency

Every 3 Months

90 days gap required between whole blood donations.
